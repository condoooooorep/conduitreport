The Witcher 3 Remastered is out today, free for owners: release times, size, and what's new

The Witcher 3 Remastered is a free update for existing owners, out 29 September. Release times, download size, and the new skill tree, visuals and photo mode.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is eleven years old, and today it gets another major overhaul. The Witcher 3 Remastered went live on 29 September, and if you already own the game, you don't pay for it.

CD Projekt Red's community manager, Marcin Momot, said the studio "never considered charging current owners." Everything below is from CD Projekt Red's own release details and patch notes.

When and where

Release time

29 September at 6 AM EDT / 3 AM PDT / 11 AM BST / 12 PM CEST / 8 PM AEST. It rolled out worldwide within an hour or two of that.

Platforms and price

PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Switch 2. Free for existing owners. On Switch 2 it's a separate eShop product: digital owners of the Switch version get it automatically, and physical Switch owners can claim their free upgrade from today.

Download size

Around 45GB according to PC Gamer, and around 50GB on Switch 2. Because it's an update to a game you already own, there was no preload, except on Battle.net.

What's actually new

The skill system

Skills are now arranged in a tree, each with three ranks, and unlocking one requires its prerequisites.

Visuals

Native DirectX 12 with multithreading, ray-traced HairWorks, textures from the fan-made HD Reworked Project, improved skin rendering, and better lighting on grass and foliage.

Reforging and photo mode

Master craftsmen can now change the look of your gear without changing its stats, which is transmog in all but name. Photo mode now lets you place and pose characters, add lights and props, and control weather and time of day.

There's also a new accessibility preset, "Bard's Ballad," with infinite durability, unlimited breath, and a toggle for Witcher Senses, plus a Ukrainian localisation.

What it means for you

If you finished The Witcher 3 years ago, the reworked skill tree is the reason to go back. It changes how Geralt builds out over a long playthrough, and that's the part of the original most players had settled into a routine with.

If you've never played it, this is now the version to start with, and it's the same price as the game was yesterday.

And if you're trying to clear your backlog before Grand Theft Auto VI on 19 November, be warned: this is a very long game. Our autumn release rundown has the rest of the calendar.

Update, 30 September

The reviews are in: a 94 on Metacritic, level with the top score of 2026, above the original's 92. Details in our verdict.
