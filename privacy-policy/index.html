Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Conduit Report ("we", "us", "the site") handles information in connection with your use of this website. By using the site, you agree to the practices described in this policy and in our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

Information we collect

The site does not require registration and provides no accounts, login system, newsletter, or comment facility. We do not request your name, email address, or other personal information as a condition of access.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address and the contents of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry, is retained only for as long as necessary for that purpose, and is not added to any mailing list or shared with third parties.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set first-party cookies for tracking or analytics. Cookies that may be placed while you use the site fall into the following categories:

  • Advertising cookies. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based

on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to users based on their visits to this and other sites. The DoubleClick cookie is the most common example.

  • Strictly necessary cookies. A hosting provider or content delivery network may set cookies required

to route traffic, maintain security, or prevent abuse. These do not track you across other websites.

You may disable or delete cookies, including third-party cookies, through your browser settings. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of certain third-party vendors' use of cookies by visiting aboutads.info. Disabling advertising cookies does not restrict access to any part of the site. Where required by law, a consent notice is presented before non-essential cookies are set.

Advertising

The site displays advertising served by third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors may use the technologies described above to deliver and measure advertisements. Advertising is not connected to editorial decisions, and no content on the site is paid for by advertisers.

Our hosting provider maintains standard server logs, which typically record information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This information is used for security, reliability, and the operation of the site. Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those resources from Google's servers.

Legal bases for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on our legitimate interests for security, site operation, and the delivery of content, and on your consent for non-essential cookies and personalised advertising.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, to object to certain processing, or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the site collects little information directly, requests concerning advertising data are generally best directed to the relevant third-party vendor. Other requests may be sent via the contact page.

The site is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
