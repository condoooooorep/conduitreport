GTA 6 is turning real-life Miami into Vice City: the Heat, Burger Shot, and billboards for brands that don't exist

The Miami Heat become Vice City for one night on 18 November, and GTA 6 brands are appearing across Miami. What's official, and what's been spotted.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Four weeks before launch, Grand Theft Auto VI's marketing has left the screen. The city that Vice City is based on is filling up with the game's fictional brands, and Miami's NBA team is changing its name for a night.

Some of this is officially announced. Much of it has been spotted by fans and reported, without a word from Rockstar. We've kept the two apart.

What's official

The Heat become Vice City

On 18 November at 7pm Eastern, the night before GTA 6 launches, the Miami Heat play the Milwaukee Bucks as "Vice City," according to the Heat's official page. The team will play on a court built for the night, in a "completely reimagined uniform."

On TV

The game is on ESPN, WPLG Local 10 and FM 104.3 WQAM.

The merch

A Vice City clothing range is on sale at the Heat's store, in person and online, with prices from $45 to $55.

The Heat announced the night on 2 October with the line "For one night only, the HEAT trade Miami for Vice City." Its page names GTA VI throughout, but doesn't mention Rockstar by name.

What's been spotted

Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell

Pop-ups for GTA's fictional fast-food chains have opened inside the Kaseya Center, the Heat's arena, with staff in GTA 6 uniforms, as VGC reports, citing fans' photos.

Ads for brands that don't exist

Adverts for in-game brands, including Weazel News, the radio station Chamber 106.6, Fruit, eCola and Vault Nightclub, have appeared on murals, buses, taxis, digital screens and a blimp around Miami.

The beach and the cars

Vice City-branded umbrellas and loungers on Miami Beach, digital billboards on boats, and custom cars with Vice City number plates, including one in Vice Beach Police colours.

Real GTA beer

A Florida brewery has filed labels for GTA's fictional beers, Pißwasser and Patriot, plus a Vice City lager, which suggests real versions could go on sale. Nothing has been confirmed.

VGC's report draws on social media posts from fans and fan accounts. It also notes that Rockstar reposted a post about the blimp, which is about as close to confirmation as Rockstar has come.

Why it matters

The Heat night is the clearest sign of how big this launch is: a real NBA team taking a video game's name and court for a night, on ESPN.

The rest fits the series. Burger Shot, Cluckin' Bell and Weazel News have been part of GTA's parody of American life for years. Putting them on real buses and billboards in the city the game is based on blurs the line in a way that suits a game set in a version of Miami.

It also matches what Rockstar has been doing online. The launch trailer leaned on mood and music, the radio stations brought in real stars, and now the fiction is appearing on the actual streets.

If you're in Miami

The Heat game on 18 November is the one fixed date. Most of the other sightings are unannounced, so they may change or disappear without notice. The game itself is out the next day, 19 November, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
