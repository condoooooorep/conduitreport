Ace Combat 8's verdict: an 87, and the series' best reviews in 25 years

Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve scored 87 on Metacritic and 88 on OpenCritic, second only to Ace Combat 04. Release dates, early access, and what critics said.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve isn't out until later this week, but the reviews are, and they're the best the series has had in a quarter of a century.

It's scored 87 on Metacritic and 88 on OpenCritic. The only entry rated higher is Ace Combat 04: Shattered Skies from 2001, at 89. It's also the first mainline game in seven years, since Ace Combat 7 in 2019.

What's confirmed

The scores

Metacritic 87 on PS5 and 86 on PC. The Xbox Series X page shows 90, but from only four reviews so far. OpenCritic 88.

Release dates

PC on 1 October, and PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on 2 October. Deluxe Edition owners get up to three days of early access. Developed by Bandai Namco Aces and published by Bandai Namco.

What critics praised

The campaign. Reviewers kept coming back to the single-player story, the cinematic presentation, and the dogfights themselves. GamesRadar+ said it "soars thanks to thrilling dogfights and a genuinely gripping Metal Gear-like story." Another review summed it up as elevating "the franchise to new heights with a spectacular campaign." The soundtrack gets singled out too.

The complaints

Two stand out. The first is readability: one review said it "comes tantalizingly close to perfection" but that the action "still lacks clarity and visibility." The second is what happens after the campaign, with that same review pointing to "a distinct lack of endgame content." Some critics also had issues with the story and with multiplayer, though neither dragged scores down much.

Should you buy it

Yes, if you liked Ace Combat 7 or you want a big, cinematic single-player campaign. That's where every review puts the game's strength.

Maybe wait, if you mostly play for multiplayer or want something to keep playing for months. The campaign is the draw, and several reviews say there's less to do once it's over.

It's a crowded week. The Witcher 3 Remastered landed today, and Gears of War: E-Day opens early access on 1 October.

Update, 1 October

PC players who got in early through the Deluxe Edition have rated the game Mixed on Steam. The main complaints are frame drops in heavy dogfights, ray tracing that can't be switched off, and limited support for flight sticks and controller remapping. Bandai Namco has acknowledged crash reports, advised updating GPU drivers, and said it hopes to patch before the full launch. The critic reviews above are unchanged. If you play on PC, check performance reports before buying.
