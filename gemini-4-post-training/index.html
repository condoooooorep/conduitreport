Gemini 4 is in post-training, and Google wants it out 'as soon as possible'

Google DeepMind's head says Gemini 4 is in post-training and running internally, with release hoped for well before year-end. What's confirmed.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Google's next flagship model is further along than many expected. Koray Kavukcuoglu, head of Google DeepMind, said on 24 September that Gemini 4 has entered post-training and that Google wants to release it "as soon as possible."

He said it on stage at The Information's AI Agenda Live summit, his first public appearance since taking over DeepMind in August.

What's confirmed

Post-training

Gemini 4 is in post-training, the stage after the main training run, where a model's behaviour is refined and safety testing and guardrails are added before release.

Already in use internally

Google is running Gemini 4 internally to power Antigravity, its AI coding tool.

The intent

In Kavukcuoglu's words: "Our intention is to, like — as soon as possible — to release an early post-training output because we see the results and we are excited." He said he hopes it arrives "much earlier" than the end of 2026.

He also said Google "took a little bit of a step back" from the previously teased Gemini 3.5 Pro to focus on its Flash models, and that Gemini 4 is now the priority.

What isn't confirmed

No release date, no benchmark scores, no pricing, and no list of versions. "Much earlier than year-end" is a hope from the person running the lab, not a date. Any Gemini 4 benchmark numbers circulating online right now are not from Google.

Why it matters now

Look at the week around it. OpenAI shipped GPT-6.1 Sol at DevDay, Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5 last week, and prices keep falling. Google talking publicly about a model that hasn't finished post-training is a sign of how compressed the release cycle between the big labs has become.

It's also a change in tone at DeepMind. Kavukcuoglu's first public message was about shipping products quickly, rather than the long-term research framing the lab has been known for.

Our read

Post-training can take weeks or months, and safety testing is where releases slip. Treat "as soon as possible" as a real intention, and wait for Google to name a date before planning around it. We'll post when it does.
