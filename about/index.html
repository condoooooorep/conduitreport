About Conduit Report

Conduit Report covers games, tech, and AI.

That means games across PC, console, and handheld, and the industry behind them. Phones, tablets, laptops, and wearables. Cameras, displays, and audio. Graphics cards, processors, and the silicon inside everything. The software that runs on all of it — operating systems, apps, platforms, and the security holes in them. And AI: the models, the labs building them, and the tools people actually use.

We publish news, analysis, and guides — with every substantive claim marked Confirmed, Reported, or Rumour, so you can see how solid it is before you rely on it.

The goal is simple: help readers work out what's actually true before a launch, not after.
