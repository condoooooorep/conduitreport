Anthropic is reportedly planning a November IPO at up to $2 trillion

Bloomberg reports Anthropic could start marketing its IPO the week of 9 November, at a valuation of up to $2 trillion. Anthropic hasn't commented.

Carried by established outlets, not officially confirmed.

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI models, is preparing to go public as soon as mid-November, according to a Bloomberg report on 1 October. If the reported valuation holds, it would be the largest stock market listing ever.

Anthropic hasn't commented publicly. Everything here comes from reporting, so the whole post carries a Reported stamp.

What's reported

The timing

Anthropic could begin formally marketing shares to investors the week of 9 November, with trading expected to start before Thanksgiving on 26 November.

The valuation

Prospective investors think the company could be valued at between $1.8 trillion and $2 trillion at listing.

The filing

Anthropic is reported to have filed confidentially for an IPO in June, which lets a company prepare its listing with regulators before details become public.

What isn't known

How many shares Anthropic would sell, how much it would raise, which banks are running the deal, and which exchange it would list on haven't been reported. Coverage of the plans has also cited financial figures, including fast revenue growth and very large losses, but we're not repeating specific numbers until they appear in a public filing we can check.

Why it matters

A listing at this size would make Anthropic one of the most valuable public companies in the world on its first day of trading. It would also put a public market price on the AI race for the first time at this scale. OpenAI has raised money privately, including from SoftBank and Nvidia, but isn't public.

The timing is notable for a second reason. Anthropic is one of the companies the FTC confirmed this week it is investigating over AI agent risks, and its chief executive signed the White House AI safety accord. Going public would bring new disclosure rules on top of both.

What to watch

The public filing. Before a US listing, a company has to make its registration statement public, and that document would show the real revenue, losses, and risks in Anthropic's own words. We'll cover it when it appears.
