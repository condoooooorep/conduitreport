GTA 6 editions explained: Standard or Ultimate, and what the extra $20 buys

GTA 6's Standard and Ultimate editions, what each includes, what every early buyer gets, pre-load dates, and the case for not deciding yet.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Pre-orders opened at local midnight on 25 June. There are two editions of the game and one bonus pack for early buyers, which is a simpler choice than the internet has managed to make it.

There's also one thing that isn't an edition at all. Rockstar's $399.99 Vice City Collection is a box of merchandise, and it doesn't include the game. If a listing offers a "collector's edition" with the game inside, it isn't from Rockstar.

The two editions

Standard, $79.99

The whole game: the full single-player campaign across Vice City and the wider state of Leonida, plus the online component. Nothing in the story is locked behind the higher tier.

Ultimate, $99.99

Everything in Standard, plus what Take-Two's announcement calls an "exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and action" tied to Jason and Lucia's story.

Both are on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Regional prices are set by each storefront and vary by market, so check yours rather than converting from dollars.

What the extra $20 gets you

Rockstar's Ultimate Edition listing, as itemised by PC Gamer:

  • Vehicles: a '95 Grotti Cheetah, a Shitzu Squalo, a '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with a garage, a Ganado

Retro Build, and Jason's safehouse vehicles, a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak

  • Weapons: the Hawk & Little Morgan revolver, plus personalised versions of the Girardi ES9 and Klose K17

pistols

  • Clothing: two outfit sets, Vice City Style and Goodtime Gear
  • A commission: the Classic Car Collection, a job from a collector named Wyman
  • Places: two mod shops (Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's), Sara's Unisex Salon, the Stock 305

clothing store, Electric Fang Tattoo, and the PTT Youngin$ compound, an illegal goods store

The vehicles and clothing are the easy part to judge: they're cosmetic, and the game will have plenty of non-exclusive cars and outfits. The commission and the extra places are the more interesting part, because they add things to do rather than things to own. How much they add is something nobody can judge until the game is out.

What every early buyer gets

Vintage Vice City Pack

Included with pre-orders and with any purchase made before 20 November, on either edition. It includes a '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage, vintage outfits and hairstyles (a linen suit for Jason, a red sequin dress for Lucia), and a tropical weapon pattern that nods to Tommy Vercetti's shirt.

A free month of GTA+

Digital pre-orders get one month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription, which can be redeemed straight away.

So the bonus isn't really a pre-order bonus. Buy the game on launch day and you still get the pack.

When you can play it

Pre-load from 12 November

Digital pre-orders can start downloading on 12 November, a week before launch. Boxed copies, which contain a download code rather than a disc, go on sale the same day.

That last point matters for storage. Whichever version you buy, you'll be downloading the game, so you'll need the full install space free either way. The only GTA 6 disc you can buy is the soundtrack.

Is Ultimate worth it?

No affiliate links here, so here's the actual argument.

For. The commission and the extra places are a lasting difference, not a car you drive twice. If you know you're the kind of player who loses hours in customisation and side activities, $20 for more of them is defensible.

Against. You're paying in advance for content you can't see, in a game you haven't played, from a list. Rockstar hasn't said whether you'll be able to upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later, so don't count on it either way.

Neither, yet. Nothing requires you to decide now. Digital copies can't sell out, the Vintage pack is yours if you buy before 20 November, and reviews are reported to arrive before launch. Unless you want a boxed copy, where retail stock is finite, waiting costs you nothing and gets you the verdict first.

Where to buy without getting burned

The Rockstar Store, PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, and the established retailers in your country. Prices are effectively identical; retailer discounts, where they exist, are small.

Avoid key resellers well under $79.99, anything promising PC access, and anything mentioning early access. There is no PC version announced and no early access programme, and those two claims are the hooks in most GTA 6 scams.

PC pre-orders

Grey-market listings for a PC version keep appearing. Rockstar has announced PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There's nothing legitimate to pre-order on PC.

Standard gets you the whole game and the whole story. Ultimate adds vehicles, weapons, clothing and extra activities tied to the campaign. Everyone who buys before 20 November gets the Vintage pack, and digital buyers can pre-load from 12 November.

Correction, 26 September

This piece said "There is no Collector's Edition." That's no longer true: on 24 September Rockstar announced a $399.99 collector's box, The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection, which does not include the game. We also should have noted that boxed physical copies of GTA 6 contain a download code rather than a disc, which Rockstar confirmed in June. Details in our collector's box coverage and the album and no-disc story.

Update and correction, 19 October

We've updated this guide throughout.
