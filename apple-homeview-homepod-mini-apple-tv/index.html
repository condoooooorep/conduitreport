Apple's HomeView is a $299 smart display for your home. Here's everything Apple launched

Apple launched HomeView at $299, plus a new HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K. Prices, release dates, what Siri AI needs, and why HomeView isn't sold in the EU.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Apple's "Welcome Home" launch landed on 13 October, and the reporting was right: a smart home hub, a new HomePod mini, and a new Apple TV 4K. The hub is called HomeView, and it's Apple's first device built to sit in the kitchen or on a wall and run the home.

Everything below is from Apple's own announcements for HomeView, HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K.

HomeView

The device

A 7-inch display on a speaker base, in tabletop and wall-mounted versions, both $299. Tabletop colours are silver, starlight, rose pink and space gray; the wall version comes in space gray and silver.

What it does

It recognises up to six people by face, each with their own home screen. It does FaceTime with Center Stage, Intercom and Messages, plays Apple Music and podcasts, shows up to nine home camera feeds at once, and listens for smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.

Where you can buy it

Pre-orders are open in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, India and New Zealand, with Singapore from 15 October. Apple says "Apple HomeView is not available for sale in the European Union."

HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K

HomePod mini

$149, in pink, green, blue, white and midnight, in stores 30 October. It recognises up to six household voices and gets Siri AI in a future software update.

Apple TV 4K

$299 with the A19 chip, which Apple says has up to 50% faster CPU performance than the previous model, and three far-field microphones for hands-free Siri. Paired with the new HomePod mini or a second-generation HomePod, it supports four-speaker surround sound.

Apple also announced an Apple Home Collection of accessories, arriving from December, with some in early 2027.

The Siri AI small print

This launch is built around Siri AI, so it's worth reading the conditions.

Still in beta

Siri AI on HomeView is in beta, in English, in the countries where it's sold.

You need an iPhone

Requests that use your personal context need a paired Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone or iPad running the latest software.

A future fee

Apple says some server-side features have daily usage limits, and expanded access will cost a fee in the future. It hasn't said how much.

That last point is the one to watch. A $299 device whose best features may later need a subscription is a different purchase from one that doesn't.

Why no EU

Siri AI isn't available in the EU, which Apple blames on the bloc's Digital Markets Act. HomeView is built around Siri AI, so Apple isn't selling it there at all: the DMA dispute is now keeping a whole Apple product out of Europe.

Should you buy one

Wait for reviews if you're tempted by HomeView. It's a first-generation device, Siri AI is still in beta, and the future fee is unpriced. It doesn't arrive until 13 November, so there's time.

The HomePod mini is the easy one. At $149, out on 30 October, it's an upgrade to a speaker many people already like, and it works without Siri AI.

The Apple TV 4K is for owners of older models. If you're on the second generation or later, Apple says you'll get Siri AI on your existing box too.

These arrive in a busy run for Apple, after the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo in September.
