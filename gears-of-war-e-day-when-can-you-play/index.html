Gears of War: E-Day: when you can preload, when reviews land, and when you can play

Gears of War: E-Day preloads 29 September, reviews and early access land 1 October, and it launches 6 October on Game Pass. Every time and what you need.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Gears of War: E-Day is Xbox's biggest release of the autumn, and it has four dates this week and next, not one. Here they are in order, with times.

The dates

Preload

Tuesday 29 September, at 11 AM ET / 8 AM PT / 3 PM UTC / 4 PM BST. That's 1 AM on 30 September in Sydney.

Reviews

The review embargo lifts on Thursday 1 October at 6 AM PT / 9 AM ET / 2 PM BST.

Early access

Also 1 October, at 11 AM ET / 8 AM PT / 3 PM UTC, for Premium Edition and Collector's Edition owners. That's five days ahead of launch.

Launch

Tuesday 6 October, on Xbox Series X|S and PC, and on Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass from day one.

What you need

Space. The install is 115GB on PC, and you should plan for at least 100GB on Xbox Series X|S.

An SSD on PC. It's a hard requirement, not a recommendation. If your games drive is a hard disk, move it before preload opens.

Game Pass and early access

A Game Pass subscription gets you the game on 6 October, not 1 October. If you're a Game Pass Ultimate or PC Game Pass member and want early access, the Premium Upgrade costs $30 on its own. The full Premium Edition is $99.99 and includes the game, early access, and some in-game extras.

Whether $30 for five days is worth it is the real decision here. The reviews land a few hours before early access opens on the same day, so you can read them before deciding whether to pay for the upgrade.

What it is

A prequel set fourteen years before the first Gears of War, at the start of the Locust invasion, with a young Marcus Fenix and Dom Santiago. The campaign runs about 14 hours, according to GamingBolt. It's staying on Xbox and PC, which Microsoft reaffirmed during its restructuring last week.

We'll add the review verdict once it's in. For the rest of the season, see our autumn release rundown.

Update, 2 October

The reviews are in: 88 on Metacritic from around 50 reviews, the best-reviewed Gears game since Gears of War 3. The PC version sits at 84, with reviewers flagging bugs and demanding hardware requirements. Full details in our verdict.
