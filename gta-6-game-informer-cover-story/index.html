GTA 6 is Game Informer's next cover: 14 pages, 12 new screenshots, out tomorrow

Game Informer's GTA 6 cover story goes live digitally on 29 September, with Rockstar's lead designers interviewed. What it covers, and what it can confirm.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

The next big piece of Grand Theft Auto VI information isn't coming from a trailer. It's coming from a magazine.

Game Informer revealed on 25 September that GTA 6 is the cover of its issue 382, and the digital edition goes live tomorrow, Tuesday 29 September. It's the first time since the Extended Look in August that Rockstar has handed anyone a substantial new batch of material.

What's confirmed

The feature

A 14-page cover story written by Game Informer senior associate editor Marcus Stewart, built on exclusive interviews with several of Rockstar's lead designers. Rockstar supplied the original cover artwork.

What's in it

12 new screenshots, plus detail on how Rockstar built Leonida: weather systems modelled on Florida's conditions, wildlife across land, air and sea, the research behind the setting, and the design of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

When

The digital issue goes live on 29 September. Print copies follow in the coming weeks, and Game Informer says physical availability is limited.

Why a magazine matters here

Rockstar almost never gives interviews during a campaign. Everything official so far has come through trailers, the newswire, and the Extended Look — all material Rockstar controls completely. A 14-page feature with designers answering questions is a different kind of source, and it's the closest thing to a developer deep-dive this game has had.

It's also a choice about what to talk about. Weather, wildlife, and research are systems and world-building topics, not story. That fits how Rockstar has run this whole campaign: show the place, say very little about the plot.

How we'll treat it

Anything Rockstar's designers say on the record in the feature counts as confirmed, the same as a newswire post. The 12 screenshots are official material too.

What won't count: the wave of "hidden detail" threads that will follow within hours, reading storefront signs and map edges in those screenshots. We covered how to tell a real GTA 6 leak from a fake one last week, and the same rule applies to screenshot analysis: something shown is evidence, something inferred from a corner of a frame is not.

We'll add what the feature actually confirms to this page once the issue is live. For everything Rockstar has officially shown up to now, see our timeline of every official GTA 6 reveal. The game is still dated for 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
