OpenAI DevDay 2026: GPT-6.1 Sol, always-on 'Dots' agents, and a Pro 500 tier

OpenAI's DevDay brought GPT-6.1 Sol, always-on Dots agents, a Pro 500 plan, agents that use computers, and Sign in with ChatGPT. Everything confirmed.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

OpenAI made more than 20 announcements at DevDay in San Francisco on 29 September. Most are for developers and businesses, but a few change what ChatGPT subscribers get. Everything below is from OpenAI's own recap.

The model

GPT-6.1 Sol

A major upgrade to GPT-6 Sol, which OpenAI says has "exceptionally strong performance on agentic coding, along with computer use, and professional work." OpenAI says it gets close to GPT-6 Astra's intelligence at a fifth of standard token prices. Available now in the API and to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users.

Ultrafast

A faster tier for GPT-6 Astra, up to 8 times faster in Codex (around 300 tokens a second) and up to 6 times faster in the API. A GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast is "coming soon."

The price is the story. A model close to the top one at a fifth of the cost continues the collapse in AI prices we've been tracking.

Agents

Dots

"Always-on agents built to handle everything," which learn your priorities and work in the background. Available on Pro and Business Premium, in beta for Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare.

Agents API with computer use

Agents that can operate software to complete tasks, with support for multiple agents working together. In the API, and in Codex and ChatGPT Work on Pro 500 and Enterprise.

Worth reading next to this month's news: OpenAI paused training on its most capable models after an agent escaped its sandbox, and is now shipping always-on agents and agents that drive computers to customers. Those are different systems in different settings, but the question of how agents are contained is the same one.

For ChatGPT subscribers

Pro 500

A new top plan with 25 times the ChatGPT Plus usage allowance and access to the Ultrafast tier. OpenAI's recap doesn't list its price.

Sign in with ChatGPT

Use your ChatGPT plan's allowance inside 16 partner tools, including Devin, Notion, and Vercel. For Plus and Pro users.

Work tools

ChatGPT Space for team knowledge, Pages for collaborative documents, collaborative Slides in the coming weeks, a Meetings plugin for notes, and @ChatGPT in Slack and Microsoft Teams. Mostly for Pro, Business, and Enterprise.

For developers and businesses

  • Codex: cloud environments, a refreshed CLI with voice control and an agents view, Code Review, and Codex Security Cloud for scanning GitHub repositories.
  • OpenAI Marketplace: Enterprise customers can put existing OpenAI commitments toward 32 partners, including Figma, Adobe, and Salesforce.
  • Bedrock Managed Agents: OpenAI-powered agents inside Amazon's AWS.
  • Privacy: zero-data-retention options with automated safety reviews, and Private Inference in preview.

Our read

Two things matter for most people. GPT-6.1 Sol makes near-top-tier AI much cheaper, which will show up in the apps built on it. And OpenAI is betting its next phase on agents that run all the time and operate software for you. That's where the value is, and it's also where this month's safety incidents have been.
