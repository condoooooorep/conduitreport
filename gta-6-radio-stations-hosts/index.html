GTA 6's radio stations are here, and Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and Slayer are hosting

Rockstar has revealed six GTA 6 radio stations you can preview now. Who's hosting each one, what they play, and what's still to come.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Six weeks before launch, Rockstar has shown off the part of Grand Theft Auto VI that most players will spend the most time with: the radio. On 8 October it put six stations on the game's official music page, each with a preview you can play now, along with new screenshots on the game's site.

Rockstar says GTA 6 has "the biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio and podcasts yet," with stations "dedicated to everything from Classic Rock to Talk Radio." The station names and descriptions below are Rockstar's. The real people behind the hosts' on-air names come from reporting by Rolling Stone and Pitchfork, so those carry a Reported stamp.

The six stations

Cocoteo

"Modern Latin from El Martillo and La Gata."

Back Country

"Airboat-ready renegade country from MW and Delta Dawne."

Afro Bank FM

"Amapiano, African classics, 3-step, and more from Burna and Palmsy."

The Chamber

"Crushing metal has landed in Leonida. Jump in the pit with DJ Kerry and DJ Tom."

Flash FM

"The Vice City institution that delivers the best in pop music and guaranteed grooves with Robyn and Alex."

Dirty South Classics

"A little southern hospitality from Trick and Trina, blasting regional rap and hip-hop classics."

Who's really behind the mic

The hosts

Bad Bunny and RaiNao host Cocoteo. Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey host Back Country, with Del Rey's husband, Jeremy Dufrene, as the station's roving reporter, Mud Dog Mike. Burna Boy and DJ Palms Trax host Afro Bank FM. Slayer's Kerry King and Tom Araya host The Chamber. Robyn and Alex Consani host Flash FM. Trick Daddy and Trina host Dirty South Classics, as reported by Kotaku, citing Rolling Stone and Pitchfork.

Several on-air names point the same way: MW for Morgan Wallen, Burna for Burna Boy, Palmsy for Palms Trax, Kerry and Tom for Slayer's pair, and Robyn, Trick and Trina as themselves.

What it tells you about the game

The station list is a map of Leonida. Airboat-ready country, Southern rap, modern Latin, and Flash FM, the pop station from 2002's Vice City, back by name. GTA radio has always been world-building as much as music, and these six read like the regions of a modern Florida.

It's an unusually star-heavy lineup for a game. Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and Morgan Wallen aren't just licensing songs here. They're hosting.

It's not the full list. Rockstar's own page mentions classic rock and talk radio, and neither is among these six. Consequence reports that the game will also have podcasts for Jason and Lucia. More stations may be revealed before 19 November.

Where it fits

This is separate from GTA VI: The Album, the 34-track record of original songs Rockstar announced in September. The album is a release you can buy; the stations are the in-game radio, and the album isn't the full soundtrack.

For everything Rockstar has officially released so far, see our timeline of every GTA 6 announcement. The game is still dated for 19 November on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Update, 19 October

One more detail from the reveal: Rockstar says you can listen to the radio and podcasts on foot, through earbuds connected to your character's phone, and that podcasts play on demand, so you choose the episode, as VGC reported.
