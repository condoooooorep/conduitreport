The only GTA 6 disc you can buy is the soundtrack

Rockstar's 34-track GTA VI: The Album is out 19 November on CD and vinyl. The game's boxed copy holds a download code, not a disc. What's confirmed.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

Rockstar announced GTA VI: The Album on 17 September — 34 original tracks, out on 19 November alongside the game, on streaming, CD, and vinyl. Which means that on launch day, the only thing with GTA 6 on a physical disc will be the music. The game itself won't be on one.

The album

GTA VI: The Album

34 original tracks from a range of artists, releasing 19 November on streaming services and in physical formats. Pre-orders are open for a CD at around $20, a standard two-record vinyl at around $50, and a limited-edition vinyl at $124.98. Six singles were released with the announcement, including Yung Lean's "That's It."

The limited edition is the eye-catching one: two transparent magenta records filled with blue liquid, with exclusive artwork. It has already sold out. Three versions — a CD jewel case, a splatter-edition vinyl, and the liquid-filled vinyl — are exclusive to Rockstar's official album store.

The album is a selection, not the full soundtrack. GTA 6 will also feature licensed music across its in-game radio stations, and Rockstar says more music announcements are coming.

The game: a code in a box

No game disc

Boxed physical copies of GTA 6 contain a download code, not a game disc. Rockstar told media this in June, before pre-orders opened.

That's why the album announcement landed badly with some fans. The disc question has followed the game since June — a boxed copy you still have to download, with nothing you can install, lend, or resell as a physical game. The soundtrack getting CDs and vinyl on the same day underlines it.

Backlash

Some fans and retailers have called for boycotts over the digital-only approach. How much that affects launch sales is still to be seen — forecasts for GTA 6's launch week remain enormous.

What it means for you

If you want the music, the CD and standard vinyl are still available; the limited vinyl isn't. The album releases the same day as the game.

If you were planning to buy a boxed copy for the disc, there isn't one. A boxed copy gets you a case and a code — useful as a gift or for collecting, but you'll download the game either way. That affects storage: you'll need the full install space on day one regardless of which version you buy.

If you want a collector's item for launch, Rockstar's $399.99 Vice City Collection box is the official option — and it doesn't include the game either.
