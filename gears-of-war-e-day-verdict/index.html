Gears of War: E-Day's verdict: an 88, and the best-reviewed Gears in 15 years

Gears of War: E-Day scored 88 on Metacritic, the series' best since Gears of War 3. What critics praised, what they didn't, and the PC caveat.

Stated by the maker, publisher, or platform holder.

The reviews for Gears of War: E-Day went live on 1 October, and the result is the best reception the series has had in fifteen years. It scored 88 on Metacritic from around 50 reviews. That's higher than anything The Coalition has made before, and second only to Epic's original trilogy.

What's confirmed

The score

88 on Metacritic from around 50 critic reviews, as reported by GamesRadar+ on 1 October. Most count as positive, with only a handful mixed.

Against the series

Above Gears of War 4 and Gears 5, both in the mid-80s, and Gears of War: Judgment at 79. Below the original trilogy, which scored between 91 and 94. The last Gears game to score higher was Gears of War 3 in 2011.

The PC version

Metacritic's PC page shows 84 from 19 reviews, with 17 positive and 2 mixed. PC reviewers mention recurring bugs, uneven performance, and ray tracing that can't be switched off and needs high-end hardware.

What critics praised

The combat, above everything. Pure Xbox gave it a 10 and said "this is the best the combat has felt in twenty years." GamesRadar+ called it "the best cover shooter I've played since Xbox 360," singling out a new adaptive cover system that "makes every shootout dynamically chaotic." VGC gave it an 8 and said that for gunplay, "very little beats it this year." GamingBolt gave it a 9 for the "tightest combat" in the series.

What they didn't

The story. Several reviews say it favours action over the relationship between a young Marcus and Dom, which is the reason a prequel exists in the first place. At the low end, Gamekult gave it a 5 and called it "bloated with loot and filler." Before the embargo, The Guardian accidentally published a 3 out of 5 early, calling the campaign repetitive, and then took it down.

Most reviews covered only the campaign. Multiplayer wasn't available to review before launch, so that part of the game is still unscored.

The context around it

Studio unrest

Eurogamer reports that staff at The Coalition fear layoffs after launch and worry about missing a bonus tied to the game's Metacritic score, which is due near the end of October. Its story director, Juan Vaca, was laid off before release. Microsoft declined to comment.

That makes this an awkward success. The game came out of the restructuring we covered in the Xbox layoffs and studio moves, and it's scoring better than any Gears game in fifteen years.

Should you play it

Yes, if you liked the original Gears games or want the best cover shooting around. It's on Game Pass, so if you subscribe, it costs you nothing extra from 6 October.

Check first, if you play on PC. The 84 and the performance complaints are worth reading before you buy, especially on mid-range hardware.
